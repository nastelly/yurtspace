01 / WHO WE ARE ✳

Building a place
for curiosity.

Yurt Space is a youth science hub helping school students turn their curiosity into projects they can build, test and share.

We bring together students, mentors and opportunities across aerospace, engineering and other STEM fields. Through bootcamps, competitions and student communities, we help young people take their next step—even when they are still figuring out where to begin.

Our mission is to make that support accessible beyond major cities and well-equipped schools. Where you study should not decide how far your ideas can go.

YURT in the media

The presentation records coverage by 100+ media outlets and TV channels, including Forbes Kazakhstan, Kazinform, Qazaqstan TV, 24.kz, Orda.kz and Kazpravda. These three articles follow the student projects, mentoring and growing Yurt Space community.

Next Stop: Jupiter? How Kazakh teens are shaping the future of space settlements

Interview with the student team behind the YURT space settlement project.

The 2025 team designed YURT and Icarus as concepts for sustainable space habitats near Jupiter. The story follows the students behind the projects and their route into the Space Settlement Contest.
